# EU AI Act record-keeping mapping

> **agentwatch is a mechanism that helps you meet an obligation, not a
> certification.** This mapping is not a conformity assessment, not legal advice,
> and not a statement that any system complies. It pairs each obligation with the
> agentwatch control and the command or test that evidences it, and it states
> what agentwatch does **not** provide.

Regulation version: EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689), as of 2026-10-03.

## Article 12 — Record-keeping (high-risk AI systems)

| Obligation | agentwatch control | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Automatically record events over the lifetime of the system | Hook → daemon → append-only store records every tool call and boundary | `agentwatch tail`, `agentwatch sessions` |
| Enable post-market monitoring of operation | Deterministic behavior records, drift signals, coverage reconciliation | `agentwatch coverage`, `agentwatch drift` |
| Logs enable reconstruction of the system's operation | Timeline replay with chain segment | `agentwatch replay <id>` |
| Traceability of automatically recorded events | Hash-chained store; tamper detection | `agentwatch verify-store` |
| Recording of the period of use | Session boundaries with timestamps | `agentwatch sessions` |

## Article 19 — Log retention

| Obligation | agentwatch control | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Retain logs for a period appropriate to the intended purpose | Configurable retention with tombstones (never a silent delete) | `agentwatch retention show`, `agentwatch retention apply` |
| Logs kept for at least six months where relevant | Default retention is 30 days and configurable upward; compliance profile sets 365 | `agentwatch config explain store.retention_days` |

## Article 26 — Obligations of deployers (logging)

| Obligation | agentwatch control | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Deployer keeps logs generated by the high-risk AI system | Local-first store, no egress required | `agentwatch status` |
| Logs available to authorities on request | Self-contained, verifiable evidence bundle | `agentwatch evidence <id>` |
| Operate in accordance with instructions | Consent-first install, config explainability | `agentwatch init --dry-run`, `agentwatch config explain` |

## What we do **not** provide

- **Risk management** (Art. 9) — out of scope; another tool.
- **Conformity assessment** (Art. 43) — agentwatch does not assess or certify.
- **Human oversight** (Art. 14) — agentwatch records approvals, it does not
  enforce or replace oversight.
- **Technical documentation** (Art. 11) — the evidence bundle is an input, not a
  substitute.
- **Data governance / accuracy** (Art. 10) — out of scope.

agentwatch records what the harness reports; it does not prove a reported action
occurred. See [forensic-soundness.md](../design/forensic-soundness.md).
